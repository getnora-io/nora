**`nora mirror docker` mirrors multi-platform images (#1184)** — for an image published as an index (`alpine:3.20`, and most official images), the mirror stored only the linux/amd64 manifest and then pushed an index that lists every platform and attestation, so NORA refused it: `rejecting manifest push: references an absent blob/sub-manifest (sha256:6243dec9…)` (the amd64 attestation, the second entry of the alpine:3.20 index) and `Mirror failed: 1 packages failed`. An image pinned by its index digest (`alpine@sha256:…`) failed as well, because the platform manifest was pushed under the index digest. Now every manifest an index lists, and every blob those manifests reference, is stored before the index, platform manifests under their own digests, so the tag or the pinned digest is the upstream index byte for byte and pulls on any of its platforms. An index that lists another index is followed down (at most 4 levels). Mirroring a multi-platform image therefore downloads and stores all its platforms, not one. Separately, after a `docker pull` of the image through NORA, NORA reported the cached blobs present, the mirror skipped them, and the manifest push was refused because the repository itself did not hold them; the mirror now asks NORA to mount such a blob into the repository (`POST /v2/<name>/blobs/uploads/?mount=<digest>&from=<name>`) and uploads it when the mount is not possible.
