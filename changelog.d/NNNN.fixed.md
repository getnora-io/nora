**A cached proxied image pulls while its Docker upstream is down (#NNNN)** — with the upstream unreachable, answering 5xx or skipped for its open circuit breaker, `docker pull` of an image NORA had cached failed with `every upstream registry failed to answer`. Docker 29 (containerd image store) pulls the attestation manifest that BuildKit and Docker Hub put in an image index, and looks up its signatures and attestations under the tag `sha256-<digest>` (the OCI referrers tag schema); the upstream had answered `404` for that tag, NORA kept no copy of a `404` and answered the lookup `502`, and docker ended the pull. With `serve_stale` on (the default), such a tag that NORA holds no copy of is now answered `404 MANIFEST_UNKNOWN` with `X-Nora-Stale: true` while no upstream answers, as a registry without referrers answers it, and the pull completes. Only that exact form (`sha256-` and 64 lowercase hex digits) is answered so: any other tag NORA has not cached stays `502` while the upstream is down, so a client does not conclude the image does not exist; an upstream that rate-limits NORA or refuses its credentials keeps its `429` or `502 DENIED`; with `serve_stale = false` nothing changes. The lookup's `404` says only that NORA knows of no signatures or attestations at that moment; a verifier that requires a signature still fails.
