**`ruzstd` 0.8.3 → 0.9.1 (RUSTSEC-2026-0344)** — a crafted `.zst` input under 128 KB could make `ruzstd` allocate several gigabytes at once (a compressed block's decompressed size was never checked against `Block_Maximum_Size`), crashing the process with an out-of-memory condition; denial of service only. `ruzstd` decodes zstd-compressed rpm and deb payloads. 0.9.1 enforces the per-block output bound (#1103).
