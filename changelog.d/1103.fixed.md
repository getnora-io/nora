**`ruzstd` 0.8.3 → 0.9.1 (RUSTSEC-2026-0344)** — a crafted `.zst` input under 128 KB could make `ruzstd` allocate several gigabytes at once (a compressed block's decompressed size was not checked against `Block_Maximum_Size`), crashing the process with an out-of-memory condition — a denial of service. `ruzstd` decodes zstd-compressed rpm and deb payloads. 0.9.1 enforces the per-block output bound. Fixed by @clain23.
