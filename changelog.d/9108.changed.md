**Rolling back to 1.4.2 with the new `[server]` keys** — 1.4.2 does not know `header_read_timeout`, `keep_alive_timeout`, `max_connections` and `maintenance_stall_secs`: it warns and ignores them, but under `NORA_CONFIG_STRICT=true` it refuses to start (exit status 78). Remove them from `config.toml` before a rollback, or set them through their environment variables (`NORA_HEADER_READ_TIMEOUT` and the others), which 1.4.2 does not read.
