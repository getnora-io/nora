**`server.maintenance_stall_secs` and background-task metrics** — `server.maintenance_stall_secs` (`NORA_MAINTENANCE_STALL_SECS`, 300 s, at least 90) is how long the maintenance loop may go without a finished tick before `/health` answers `503`. `nora_task_panics_total{task}` counts panics of long-lived background tasks and `nora_maintenance_timeouts_total` the maintenance ticks cut at their deadline; `deploy/prometheus-rules.yml` gains a `NoraTaskPanic` alert. See `MONITORING.md`.
