**Token lifetimes are capped** — every mint route (`POST /api/tokens`, `POST /api/v1/admin/tokens`, the web UI) now grants at most 3650 days, and a token minted by an OIDC identity lives no longer than its provider's `max_token_lifetime_secs` or what is left of its JWT, to the second, and a JWT with nothing left mints no token. Before, `ttl_days` was taken as asked (see Security). The answer of a mint carries `expires_in_secs`, the lifetime granted; `expires_in_days` stays, rounded down. Every mint leaves one `token_mint` audit entry with the actor, the owner, the role and the lifetime, never the token.
