**No temp files left behind by a shutdown or a crash (#1060)** — a SIGTERM during cache fills left `<key>.tmp.<pid>.<seq>` files beside keys for good, and a write could truncate a stored key that happened to have its temp name. Temp files are now owned by a guard and created exclusively; local writes are staged in `<root>/.nora-staging/<instance>/`, which the next start removes for instances that are gone (a second instance on the same volume keeps its own); and streamed `raw` uploads cut off by a crash are swept from `tmp/raw-uploads` after 4 hours idle.
