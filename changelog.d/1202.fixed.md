**The Terraform module download goes through the circuit breaker (#1202)** — `GET /terraform/v1/modules/{ns}/{name}/{provider}/{version}/download` asked its upstream with a bare HTTP client, past the `terraform` circuit breaker that every other Terraform path reports to: an open breaker did not stop module downloads (each one waited on a dead upstream and answered `502`), and a failing download never counted toward opening it. The download now reports to the breaker: an open breaker answers `503` with `Retry-After` without asking the upstream, a 5xx, network error or timeout counts as a failure, and, as on the other Terraform paths, a 5xx or a network error is retried once before NORA answers. A `4xx` is still `404`, and a reply without `X-Terraform-Get` is still `502`. `terraform init` honours the `Retry-After` of a `503`, so with the breaker open it waits about 30 seconds before it fails, as it already did on the other Terraform paths.
