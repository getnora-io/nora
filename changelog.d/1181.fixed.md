**A proxied artifact served right before a stop is kept (#1181)** — A graceful stop now waits for the proxy cache writes that run after their response, within the same 10 seconds it already gave the background schedulers, so an artifact served right before the stop is kept and is served after a restart even without the upstream. The total stop time does not grow. A write still running at the end of those 10 seconds is named in a warning and fetched from the upstream again later.
