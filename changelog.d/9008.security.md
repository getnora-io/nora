**Connections without a request were held open indefinitely (GHSA-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX)** — an HTTP/1 connection that never finished its request head, and an HTTP/2 connection that opened no stream or went idle after one, were kept open with no deadline, so an unauthenticated client could exhaust connections. A connection now has `server.header_read_timeout` (30 s) for its request head; an HTTP/2 connection must open its first stream within it and is closed with `GOAWAY` after `server.keep_alive_timeout` (120 s) without one — keep-alive pings do not extend it — and `server.max_connections` bounds open connections. See Added.
