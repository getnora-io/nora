**Token mints were not held to a lifetime ceiling (GHSA-54qr-w49h-mgfq)** — `POST /api/tokens` and `POST /api/v1/admin/tokens` passed `ttl_days` to the store unchecked: a large value made a token that never expires and a larger one wrapped around to a few hours; a token an OIDC identity minted could outlive its JWT, even one already expired within the clock leeway. Every mint route now takes its lifetime from one rule (see Changed) and is audited.
