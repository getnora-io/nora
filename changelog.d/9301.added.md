**Connection limits in `[server]`** — `header_read_timeout` (`NORA_HEADER_READ_TIMEOUT`, 30 s: the time a client has to send a request head, and for HTTP/2 to open its first stream), `keep_alive_timeout` (`NORA_KEEP_ALIVE_TIMEOUT`, 120 s: how long an idle connection, or an HTTP/2 connection without streams, waits before it is closed) and `max_connections` (`NORA_MAX_CONNECTIONS`, 4096: past it new connections wait in the listen backlog; keep it below the open-file limit). A `keep_alive_timeout` of 90 s or less starts with a warning: a reverse proxy keeping idle backend connections longer (traefik 90 s, nginx 60 s) would send requests into connections NORA is closing.
