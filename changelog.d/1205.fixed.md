**Registry reads and writes are rate limited separately, as documented (#1205)** — every registry request, a `cargo` download or a sparse-index read as much as a push, spent the `upload_*` bucket, while `general_*` only added a second limit to `/api/tokens`, so raising `NORA_RATE_LIMIT_GENERAL_*` did nothing for the 429s of a cold `cargo install` or `dotnet restore`. Now writes (PUT, POST, PATCH, DELETE) spend `upload_*` and reads (GET, HEAD, OPTIONS, and npm's audit POST) spend `general_*`, each its own bucket per client, so reading hard no longer starves a client's own publishes, nor the other way round; auth routes answer to `auth_*` alone. The `general_*` defaults rise from 100/s, burst 200 to 1000/s, burst 2000. Until 1.5 a read gets the larger of `general_*` and `upload_*`, setting by setting, so a configuration that kept `general_*` below `upload_*` — the helm chart's 100/200, the documented presets, an `upload_*` raised to cure read 429s — reads no harder than before, and NORA warns at startup; from 1.5 reads are limited by `general_*` alone. `Rate limits enforced` in the startup log now reports `write_*` and `read_*` in place of `upload_*` and `general_*`.
