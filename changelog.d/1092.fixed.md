**pub packages with capitals in their name are served (#1092)** — NORA answered 400 to a pub package name with a capital letter, while pub.dev holds a few such packages (`Pong`, `RAL`, `Flutter_Nectar`) and `dart pub get` asks for them as written. Names now follow pub.dev's own rule, `[a-zA-Z_][a-zA-Z0-9_]*`.
