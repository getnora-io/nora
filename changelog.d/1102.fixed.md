**OIDC: one JWKS refresh at a time, with a backoff after a failure (#1102)** — after a restart every request carrying an OIDC token ran discovery and a JWKS fetch on its own, and a failed fetch was not remembered, so under load the herd repeated until one fetch got through; when discovery timed out the fallback `{issuer}/.well-known/jwks.json` was fetched, which GitHub's issuer answers 404 (its JWKS is at `/.well-known/jwks`), and about 1000 requests were rejected in 5 minutes on one instance. Now one request refreshes the JWKS while the others wait for its result, a failed fetch with nothing cached makes the next 5 s of requests fail fast (stale keys are still served when present), and the JWKS URI from a successful discovery is reused on later refreshes.
