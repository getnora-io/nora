**A proxied Docker miss carries an OCI error the client prints (#1189)** — when no upstream had a manifest or blob, NORA answered a bare `404` (or `502`/`503`) with an empty body, and `docker pull` printed only `Error response from daemon: unknown:`, so a missing tag could not be told from a broken proxy. Every miss on the proxy path now answers `application/json` with an OCI error: `404 MANIFEST_UNKNOWN` (with the name and reference) or `404 BLOB_UNKNOWN` (with the digest), as a miss of a pushed image already did; `502 UNAVAILABLE` when every upstream failed; `503 UNAVAILABLE` with `Retry-After` when an upstream was skipped for its open circuit breaker; and, as before, `502 DENIED` when an upstream refused NORA's credentials. `docker pull` now prints `manifest unknown`; dockerd does not print the body of a 5xx.
