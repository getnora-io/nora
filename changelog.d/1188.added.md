**Docker upstreams can own namespaces (#1188)** — `namespaces = ["team-*"]` on a `[[docker.upstreams]]` entry binds names by their first path segment (an exact segment, or a stem followed by `*`) to that upstream. A bound name is asked of that upstream only, with the whole path (no prefix stripping, no `library/` retry), its copy is stored under that upstream's namespace, and whatever the upstream answers is final: before, under `default_action = "allow"`, a 401 or a missing tag at the private registry let the next upstream serve a same-named public image. An upstream with `namespaces` is no longer asked for any other name, under either `default_action`. `NORA_DOCKER_PROXIES` takes them as a fourth field, `url|auth|prefix|team-*;ops`. Overlapping namespaces, a namespace that holds for a `prefix`, and an entry that is not a segment or a stem with `*` refuse to start. Downgrading to 1.4.3 or older ignores `namespaces` and puts the private registry back into the fallback chain; switch those names to an explicit host (`prefix` and `default_action = "deny"`) before downgrading.
