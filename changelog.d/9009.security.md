**An upstream URL rewrite could point a client at another host (GHSA-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX)** — the npm, Ansible Galaxy and pub rewrite of the upstream's URLs into NORA's was a plain substring replace, so it also fired where the upstream URL was only a prefix of a longer host, path segment, port or userinfo (`https://<upstream>.attacker.example/…`); depending on the replacement, the result led to another host or to a mangled path on NORA. The rewrite now replaces only where the upstream's URL ends, in the plain and the JSON-escaped form, and an npm version whose tarball lies outside the upstream is left out of the documents NORA serves. NuGet was not affected.
