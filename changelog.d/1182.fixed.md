**Downloads no longer wait out the object store's retries while the store is down (#1182)** — with S3 or GCS storage stopped or not answering, a download waited for the store client's retries before NORA answered: about 70 seconds to a minute or more, or until the client gave up when the store took connections but never answered, and then often with a 404 or 500 that clients take as final. A read now waits at most `[storage] answer_timeout_secs` (default 10, env `NORA_STORAGE_ANSWER_TIMEOUT_SECS`) for the store to answer; a body that has started streams at its own pace as before. A request the store left unanswered is answered `503 Service Unavailable` with `Retry-After: 30` instead of the 404, 500 or 502 it would have had; any other answer, including what a proxy served from its upstream, is unchanged. While the store has not answered, proxied artifacts are served without being cached and writes are refused at once with 503, until the store answers again. `/ready` is unchanged. Set `answer_timeout_secs = 0` to wait as long as the store client retries, as 1.4.5 did, for example for a slow store that is recovering.
