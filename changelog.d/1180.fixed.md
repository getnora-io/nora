**npm: tarball links follow the instance that serves them (#1180)** — a packument NORA keeps names each tarball at the `public_url` of the instance that wrote it. A store moved to another address sent `npm install` without a lockfile to the old one: `ECONNREFUSED` on 1.4.2, and `ETARGET` from 1.4.3, where the versions with links outside NORA and the upstream are left out. Now a link of NORA's own form to a tarball NORA holds moves to the serving instance's address, in the cached packument and in published ones, when the link is of this instance's origin or the stored bytes are the digest the packument declares for that version; a file of the same name from another source is not taken for the link. A version whose tarball NORA does not hold stays out of the packument while the upstream is down, as before.
