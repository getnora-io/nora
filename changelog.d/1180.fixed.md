**npm: tarball links follow the instance that serves them (#1180)** — a packument NORA keeps names each tarball at the `public_url` of the instance that wrote it. A store moved to another address sent `npm install` without a lockfile to the old one: `ECONNREFUSED` on 1.4.2, and `ETARGET` from 1.4.3, where the versions with links outside NORA and the upstream are left out. Now a link of NORA's own form to a tarball NORA holds moves to the serving instance's address, in the cached packument and in published ones, when the link names this instance's current address or the address NORA wrote into the copy when it stored it. A copy stored by an earlier release has no such record: its links to another address are external, and the packument is fetched again while the upstream answers, which writes the record. A version whose tarball NORA does not hold stays out of the packument while the upstream is down, as before.
