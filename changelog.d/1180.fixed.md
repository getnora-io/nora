**npm: tarball links follow the instance that serves them (#1180)** — a packument NORA keeps names each tarball at the `public_url` of the instance that wrote it. A store moved to another address sent `npm install` without a lockfile to the old one: `ECONNREFUSED` on 1.4.2, and `ETARGET` from 1.4.3, where the versions with links outside NORA and the upstream are left out. Now a link of NORA's own form to a tarball NORA holds moves to the serving instance's address, in the cached packument and in published ones; a link to a tarball NORA does not hold is left out as before.
