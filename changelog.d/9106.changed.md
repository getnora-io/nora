**npm tarballs come only from where the upstream's packument says** — a tarball is fetched only for a version the upstream's packument lists, at the link it names; a version it does not list is `404` with no request for the tarball. A redirect on the upstream's origin is followed with its credential; one to another origin (GitHub Packages keeps files on a storage host) is followed without it, at most ten redirects for the whole chain within one `npm.proxy_timeout`, the eleventh is `502`. A `proxies` entry bound to scopes answers their metadata only from its own origin: a redirect off it is `502`. A version whose tarball lies on another host is left out of the documents NORA serves, with `latest` falling back to the highest release still listed; a tarball a GitLab instance-level endpoint keeps under its project, on the upstream's origin, is served through NORA.
