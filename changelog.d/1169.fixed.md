**A stored Docker blob is verified before its last byte goes out (#1169)** — a blob served from storage was hashed as it streamed and its digest checked when the reader reached the end, but with `Content-Length` declared hyper stops reading once that many bytes are out, so the check never ran: a blob whose bytes changed on disk reached the client as a complete `200`. Docker clients reject such a layer by its digest; curl and scripts did not. Stored blobs, and blobs served while a fill is in flight, now go through the verifying stream raw and Hugging Face files use: the last frame is held back until the digest is checked, so a mismatch ends the body one frame short and is logged.
